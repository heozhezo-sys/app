# FEATURES MASTER

## Core
Today dashboard, habits, goals, tasks, calendar, notifications and analytics.

## Body
Gym, home workout, running, walking, cycling, swimming, sports, mobility, stretching, yoga and recovery.

## Health
Sleep, hydration, nutrition and optional HealthKit integration.

## Mind
Focus timer, journaling, reading and personal reviews.

## Books
Local PDF import, library, reader, progress, bookmarks, highlights, notes and reading analytics.

## Money
Income, expenses, budgets, accounts and savings tracking.

## Growth
Achievements, streaks, milestones, yearly review and personal progress score.

All modules are optional and can be hidden from the main dashboard.
