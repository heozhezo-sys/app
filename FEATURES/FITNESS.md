# FITNESS

## Activities
Gym, home workout, running, walking, cycling, swimming, sports, mobility, stretching, yoga and recovery.

## Workout model
Workout → exercises → sets/intervals → metrics → session summary.

Track reps, weight, duration, distance, pace, rest, RPE and notes where applicable.

## Plans
Support templates, weekly plans, custom schedules, warm-ups, supersets, circuits, rest periods and recovery days.

## History
Every session is retained for analytics. Derive totals from session records instead of manually maintained lifetime totals.

## Health boundary
Fitness guidance is informational and must not claim to diagnose or treat medical conditions.
